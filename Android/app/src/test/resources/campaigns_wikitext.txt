'''Campaigns''' (also known as '''Galactic War Campaigns''') are supplements to the Galactic War system in ''[[Helldivers 2]]'' that narratively link [[Major Orders]] together. They are organized via names, and revolve around events pertaining to a faction's activities. Upon completion, participants are rewarded with a [[stratagems|Stratagem]], Weapon, or Armor/Cosmetic. 
__TOC__

== Overview ==
Each Campaign contains at least one Major Order and has a unique name, such as "Census Thunder". Like Major Orders, Campaigns require the combined efforts of the majority of the Helldiver population to succeed. Each successful Campaign grants at least one reward if conditions are met and are considered successful if a majority of Major Orders within it are completed.

Each Campaign so far has lasted around 2 weeks, with each having 3 different phases.

=== Campaign Rewards ===
Campaign Rewards are distributed to all participating Helldivers who partake in the Campaign, similarly to how Major Orders distribute {{Currency|Medals}}. Since they are typically three Major Orders tied together, participating in at least one MO is guaranteed to be rewarded with one at the end. Currently, Campaign rewards can consist of:

== Trivia ==
* While the old Major Order system did something similar, Campaigns connect them more coherently by linking MOs together via Operation names, typically with three MOs under a single campaign name. While it is possible for a Campaign to involve multiple factions and their fronts, all of them are related to its overarching goal.
** Prior to this system, Major Orders would frequently rotate between factions, typically with one Major Order per faction and occasionally on the same front in a row.
** While Campaigns can involve other fronts, their overall objective(s) are narratively involved with the Campaign Faction focus (i.e. Gather materials for the [[TCS+]] on the [[Automaton]] and [[Illuminate]] fronts).
